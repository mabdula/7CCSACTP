\begin{isamarkuptext}%
Another standard way to prove facts is by \isa{case\ analysis}. In this method you prove a theorem
      by showing that the theorem's statement holds for a finite number of cases. Those cases
      need to be exhaustive, i.e.\ at least one of has to hold.

      In the following example, the proof is by considering two cases: \isa{x\ {\isasymge}\ y} and \isa{{\isasymnot}\ x\ {\isasymge}\ y}. This
      is done in the step case.%
\end{isamarkuptext}\isamarkuptrue%
