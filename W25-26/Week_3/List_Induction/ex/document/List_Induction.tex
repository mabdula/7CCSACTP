\begin{isamarkuptext}%
In analogy to natural numbers, facts about lists can be proved by induction. However, instead
      of the base case being \isa{{\isadigit{0}}}, we use the empty list \isa{{\isacharbrackleft}{\kern0pt}{\isacharbrackright}{\kern0pt}}, and the step case is proved for \isa{x\ {\isacharhash}{\kern0pt}\ xs}
      instead of \isa{Suc\ n}. The induction principle is 

  \[\frac{P\; [];\;\;\; [\forall\; x.\; P\; xs;\; \Longrightarrow\; P\; x#xs]}{P\; t}.\]

The following is a proof of the associativity of Isabelle's list append \isa{{\isacharat}{\kern0pt}}. Reproduce it and note
base case and the step-case. In the base case we almost always unfold defining equations and in the
step-case we always manipulate the goal until the induction hypothesis is applicable.%
\end{isamarkuptext}\isamarkuptrue%
